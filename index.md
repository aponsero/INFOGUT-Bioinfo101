Advances in in vitro gut models, high-throughput sequencing and metabolomics are transforming our understanding of host-microbiome interactions. These technologies generate increasingly complex datasets, and researchers need computational skills to analyse them in a robust, reproducible and biologically meaningful way.

This website gathers self-study tutorials covering the fundamental bioinformatics skills needed to analyse microbiome and metabolomics data from in vitro colon models. The material was first taught at the [Third INFOGUT Training School](https://aponsero.github.io/INFOGUTWG4-TrainingSchool-2026/) (IMDEA Nutrition, Madrid, September 2026), organised by Working Group 4 of the COST Action INFOGUT.

## Who it is for

The course is designed for PhD candidates, postdoctoral researchers and technical staff who work with in vitro colon models, or are planning to. It starts from the very beginning: **no prior programming experience is required**.
